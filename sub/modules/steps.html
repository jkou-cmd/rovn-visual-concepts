Compare the whole offer.

Pay basis, hours, benefits, orientation and conditions. Write down what’s unclear and ask.

Ask for the path to day one.

Who owns each remaining step, what you need to send, and the start date it’s all working toward.

Keep what stays useful.

Documents, receipts and expiry dates, kept together for the next time someone asks.
