Colour

Three brand colours. White is every surface, ink carries text and action, amber is light: hover, focus on dark, progress and motion. It never fills a resting surface and never carries text on white. Click any swatch to copy its token.

Space, shape, depth, motion

A 4px base. Depth comes from tone and shadow, not outlines: no hairline dividers, no bordered cards. The one exception is form fields, which need a visible boundary to pass contrast.

Motion

Ease-out --ease-out for movement, --ease-spring with --dur-spring for hover swells and toggles, --hover-scale 1.04 and --press-scale 0.97 for filled buttons. Reduced motion collapses every duration to 1ms.

Buttons

Ink pill for the one primary action on a screen. Secondary is tone, never an outline. On hover every filled button does the same thing, light or dark: it swells slightly on a spring, its fill steps one tone, and a trailing arrow slides forward. Amber stays out of resting and hover states; it appears as light in motion, like the glint when a save lands.

On ink

On dark surfaces the primary inverts to white with the same hover. Quiet buttons get a faint white tint, and focus turns amber (7.4:1 on ink).

Sizes

Small buttons keep a 44px hit area through padding on touch screens. Labels are verbs that match where the button goes.

Forms

Labels live inside the field and lift as you type, so a form stays short without hiding what each box is for. Checks happen quietly as you go; problems surface only when you try to continue, and say exactly what to fix.

    Focus

    • On white: 2px ink outline, 2px offset, soft amber halo. The ink ring carries the contrast; the halo is decoration.
    • On ink: 2px amber outline (7.4:1).
    • Amber alone is never a focus indicator on white. It measures 2.4:1 and fails the 3:1 minimum.
    • Focus is never removed. Mouse clicks don't show it (:focus-visible); keyboard always does.

    Labels and forms

    • Every field has a real label. It rests inside the field and lifts to a caption; it never disappears, and there is no placeholder text to mistake for a value.
    • Help and error text are linked with aria-describedby; invalid fields set aria-invalid.
    • On a failed submit, focus moves to an error summary with links to each field.
    • Errors use an icon and words as well as colour.
    • Field boundaries are 3.4:1. This is the one deliberate exception to the no-outlines rule.

    States and announcements

    • Loading sets aria-busy with a polite status message, so screen readers hear what's loading.
    • Errors use role="alert". Success uses role="status".
    • Forbidden renders nothing from the protected data: no names, counts or totals.
    • Every non-ready state has at least one action except loading.

    Motion, targets, type

    • prefers-reduced-motion collapses durations to 1ms and stops the skeleton shimmer.
    • Targets are at least 44px on touch; default buttons and fields are 48px.
    • Reading text is 13px minimum; 12px is for chips only.
    • Display serif is used at 24px and up. Below that, Inter.
    • Text scales to 200% without loss; everything is in rem.
