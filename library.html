Calculator method

Every result should
show its workings.

Cash, capacity and contribution are calculated separately, and cost has to be stated. Here’s how to read the calculator, and how to challenge it.

Agency premium is a difference.

Cash avoided is the agency hours you actually replace, multiplied by the difference between the agency rate and your fully loaded staff rate. You can only replace hours you have people for.

Capacity is a different result.

Time returned to your credentialing team is capacity. You can put a labor rate on it, but it isn’t money saved unless spending actually goes down, so the calculator never adds it to cash.

Contribution is not revenue.

Contribution is what the work adds once its costs are taken out. It is shown on its own line and never counted as cash.
